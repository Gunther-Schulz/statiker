## 3. The class's mechanism

Every grading instrument in this system — attack round, verify
leg, checkpoint review — grades a FROZEN object (pinned artifact,
lock sha, reviewed state). The repairs to its findings are
composed AFTER it, enter the record carrying discharged-the-
finding authority, and no fresh context has read them. That seam
is structural: any added round mints its own post-round surface
(its repairs), so the class cannot be closed by adding rounds —
only priced per lap. (DERIVED from the series shape 6→2→1: the
iterated round DOES converge on the class, at one round's latency
per lap.)

The post-round surface divides by CLAIM KIND, and the division is
what the evidence discriminates:

(a) MECHANICAL repairs — a prescribed form executed. The
    executed red/green pair's reach matches the claim. Residual
    defect mode: side effects in the silent direction (RB1 → N2).
    A pair cannot certify reach (corpus: red certifies the class
    that fired); the next round's ordinary read is the catcher,
    and it caught both instances in evidence.
(b) DESIGN-BEARING repairs — the repair itself decides something
    new: a predicate's shape, a new hold code, an amended
    requirement, an exclusion boundary. An executed pair proves
    the mechanism FIRES as designed, never that the design is
    right — the pair is derived from the repair's own design, the
    same-parentage shape (corpus, Parentage). RN-b is the clean
    incident: pair present, executed, green — design wrong, no
    field incident behind it, withdrawn on a field reading.
(c) ACCOUNTS — prose justifying a boundary or figure (F113,
    F110). No executable check exists; only a fresh reader
    catches falsity.

Answer to the P28 body's sub-question (the judgment/mechanization
boundary): closure claims about STATE — a count, a hold cleared, a
check's outcome — must and already do carry executed evidence
(SKILL.md b6d5beb: reach-matched evidence block, the attack
question; F175's executed-red mandate, Implementation; the
per-finding red-record demand, Verify). Closure claims about
DESIGN SOUNDNESS or JUSTIFICATION cannot carry discriminating
executed evidence even in principle — for them the pair is
same-parentage — so mandating executed evidence there buys
false-clean, not coverage. The boundary is the CLAIM's kind, and
the mechanizable act at the seam is DECLARING the kind, not
grading it.

   prod remain its floor (efficiency convention: the closing
